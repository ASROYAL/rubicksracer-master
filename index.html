How to play
  • Everyone gets the same shuffled 5×5 board and the same 3×3 target pattern (top-left of each panel).
  • Slide tiles into the empty gap until the centre 3×3 square of your board matches the target. First to finish wins.
  • Keys slide a tile in that direction into the gap (tick Invert keys to move the gap instead). You can also click or tap a tile next to the gap.
  • Esc pauses. Four people on one keyboard works, but some keyboards can't register that many keys at once.
